# Power BI Day 27 — Installation and First Report

## Topics Learned
- Installed Power BI Desktop.
- Loaded the built-in Financial Sample dataset.
- Explored the `financials` table and its fields.
- Created a horizontal bar chart for Total Sales by Country.
- Created a column chart for Total Profit by Country.
- Added a Country slicer to filter both charts interactively.
- Saved the Power BI report as `Day-27-Financial-Analysis.pbix` on the D: drive.

## Initial Observations
- The United States of America has the highest total sales in the displayed report.
- France has the highest total profit in the displayed report.
- Mexico has comparatively lower sales and profit.

## Key Learning
Power BI transforms data into interactive reports. Visuals can be filtered using slicers, allowing users to explore business performance by category.
