
# Day 43 – Business Analysis Using Power BI

## Objective
Configure visualizations to answer important business questions.

## Visualizations and Field Configuration

### 1. Sales and Profit by Country
- Visual: Clustered Bar Chart
- Y-axis: Country
- X-axis: Total Sales, Total Profit

### 2. Sales by Product
- Visual: Bar Chart
- Y-axis: Product
- X-axis: Total Sales

### 3. Sales by Segment
- Visual: Donut Chart
- Legend: Segment
- Values: Total Sales

### 4. Monthly Sales Trend
- Visual: Line Chart
- X-axis: Month Name
- Y-axis: Total Sales

## Business Questions
- Which country generates the highest sales and profit?
- Which products contribute most to revenue?
- How is sales distributed across customer segments?
- How do sales change over time?

## Learning Outcomes
- Learned how to configure chart field wells.
- Practiced comparative business analysis.
- Understood categorical and numerical data visualization.
- Learned how to select appropriate charts for business questions.
